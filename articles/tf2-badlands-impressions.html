TF2 Badlands Impressions

November 20, 2026

I just finished a game of control points on badlands and I don’t know how long it took, maybe twenty minutes.

I committed to pyro despite there being maybe four engineers on the enemy team at a blockade that was far enough away that I couldn’t do anything.

Ended the game with decent damage, less than decent k/d.

The main thought I had playing the game was,

Holy shit,These games on tf2 take so long.

You really get to know the lobby.

I mean, I could go through all five stages of grief and eat a packet of starkist tuna creations hot buffalo and still be in the same match with these goofy guys.

In tf2, I’ve learned not to read the chat, because you have some seriously fucked up chats in there.

But apart from that, the usernames are almost always golden.

We got Dickless Pidgeon,

We got a player who somehow had his name be blank,

We got a player named player,

And we got lobotomy enthusiast.

And this is just in one game, one lobby.

I also remembered that I’d set my sensitivity to all source engine games to exactly 2.

And I also noticed how fast I felt I was moving in game, compared to games I’d played recently on an older 360 console for some custom games.

Compared to Smash Ultimate, TF2 doesn’t have an egregiously fast pace. In fact, I’d say they are comparable in their pace. They also have competitive scenes that take advantage of mastery over the pace.

I also remembered how much I hate the reticle for Pyro’s melee weapon.

I usually don’t like TF2’s maps because they’re all samey

(and I just don’t like the red rocks / wooden structure of north america aesthetic, I’m sorry guys)

But I was able to ignore the map and just focus on the gameplay, which was pretty fun.

In one map you have enough claustrophobic chokes to sit and get kills as pyro.

You have areas to sneak around back and surprise the enemy.

You have a surprising amount of space to get out of an enemy’s way.

I thought I was going to have a difficult time as pyro but I did fine. I think I ended up about middle of the scoreboard at the end, and in the beginning I was doing pretty hot.

I also got a handful of achievements!

Yay!
