How Apex Legends Players use Roles

November 22, 2026

In Apex legends, in multiplayer, when you wanna actually do good, there are some things people figured out you gotta do.

Now I don’t know, if this is everybody centering on the optimal strategy, or if it’s just people kind of doing what everyone else is doing and in the future new ways of doing things are going to emerge and this way of doing things will seem so outdated, and worse-performing.

But in any case this is how things shook out.

So you want a shotcaller. A person who makes the decisions. This is called the IGL. They are the role of the classic captain of the team. They are the one who says, we go here, we do this, attack, retreat, loot. Everyone follows them.

But they aren’t the best at getting kills. They are good, but they aren’t the main person on the team who gets kills. That is a role called the fragger. A frag is a classic multiplayer gaming term that means ‘kill’. It comes from military slang, originally (wow, there’s a lot of military culture in gaming.) In gaming, I used to think it originated in Counter Strike lobbies, but actually it’s been used as early as Doom and Quake. So it’s been around as long as multiplayer gaming has.

So fraggers are usually a class/character (in Apex, they’re called ‘Legends’) that has offensive abilities.

So you’ve got a fragger and an IGL but what about the third person? In Apex, a squad is three people. And the third person’s role comes about because of how the game is kind of played.

So, imagine you’re playing Apex and you are sitting pretty on a nice spot. It’s going to be in the next ring, it’s high ground … you like it.

But you’ve already looted it, and there’s a nice spot in the distance that has nice loot, and maybe a team you can go kill.

Well, you wanna go there, but you can’t risk losing your spot. So one player hangs back. They have a long-range weapon like a sniper, and while you and one other player goes forward to loot and kill, that hang-back player sits there and holds the spot.

If another team sneaks up and tries to steal the spot, since you have a player hanging back they can be like “yo someone is trying to steal the spot come back!” and you can retreat back to your spot.

And so the role ‘anchor’ was born. An anchor is the hang back player, the person who can be slow and have defensive abilities because their job is to just sit and protect an area. If it’s a good area, you might call it a ‘strong position’.

Gibraltar is a popular anchor. He has a lot of health and he is slow.
