The same 2 Battlefront IIs 2 Decades Apart

November 16, 2026

One Tuesday in October, I played Battlefront II on the PS5. This was the second Battlefront II I had played (ironically). The first one was before 2006, back on the original Xbox at my friend’s house. Usually, I played the original Battlefront II on PC at another friend’s house.

Okay, so we’ve got two different versions of a game played at three different locations on three different consoles across twenty years.

And they are the same.

In all that time, the gameplay didn’t change, nor did it get any less interesting. The game design is simple:

Big map. Each team has a total amount of lives. You kill the enemy and the first team to finish the other team’s final life wins.

Battlefront’s gimmick is that you have a few classes you start out playing, then you unlock more powerful classes as you get points in the game, then when you get more points you unlock the third and final tier: jedi, sith, and special characters with guns and jetpacks.

And it’s as fun as when I first played it. I loved playing against a buddy, and then we both have a team of inept AI on our team so that we can just shoot the rest and avoid the opposing player who is good, and it feels like we’re cleaning house.

You feel powerful, and if the players are similarly matched then the match is going to be close. You can fight each other in high-intensity battles but you don’t have to keep your blood level high the whole match because you’re fighting mostly against AI.

It is intense, and exciting, especially when it comes down to the wire with both teams having a low amount of lives. It also feels great to come back from a deficit, especially if you made it happen.

It feels great to level up and unlock more powerful characters that are fun to play. Droidekas are fun as hell to smash face with, while you’re invulnerable behind that shield.

I’d like to make a note: I do not like Star Wars.
