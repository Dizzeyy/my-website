What I Like in Game Design

November 9, 2026

I once read a book I loved by Jesse Schell called the Art of Game Design
Which on paper is the kind of book I would not like because it's more a collection of lots of tiny LITTLE ideas without overarching design principles, but it's fun and it looks good and she says a lot of stuff that is just - good, like good game design
But also it's overwhelming, because - it's a fire hose of different game design principles getting blasted at your face at 1,000 mph and it's like holy shit

That's the thing. I don't like designing video games. But I like designing narrative rpg games. I really enjoy making D&D campaigns, or enjoyed. D&D is a mixed bag for me.

Game design is something I've been into since I watched the developer commentary on Portal. Now, the aesthetic of portal really turns me off. The whole source engine aesthetic is just not my vibe. Too grungy and dark and - like - scratchy? It's not lovely.

I love juicier, friendlier aesthetics like Overwatch. Blizzard does a grand job at making their stuff look pretty. I could never play TF2 because it just didn't look as good or as interesting as Overwatch. All the maps are just North American / Yukon backdrop, except for a few. I had the same problem in Halo Reach, where so many of the maps in the multiplayer rotation had the same palette: the forge world palette. So whether you're playing on ascension or blood gulch or asylum, you're playing on grey metal structs on a green background and blue sky.

every.

fucking.

time.

Got so old.

Halo 3, each map is so different. I care so much about the aesthetics of the game.

Which is too bad from a game design perspective, because for each volume you could write on designing specific mechanics and how that affects gameplay and game theory, there's just a line or two to write about aesthetics.

And it will just come down to something like fuckin

"just make it look good lol"

I mean you can talk about pallettes and color design

but here's the thing, that's not that interesting to me

I've never sat through an hour video about color schemes and visual aesthetics

I just like them

But I've watched idk how many GMTK videos

And I used to re-watch Dunkey's analysis video essays, like his dunkviews and videos like "Video game structure" (even though that one is kinda weak tbh)

So yeah. I like to role play as someone into game design but I don't like designing video games and the only game I actually like to design is so fundamentally frustrating I gave it up.

But there's more to game design than making video games, there's game theory, and talking about the theory of specific games whose theory and mechanics don't really apply to anything else, like niche card games or Minecraft or whatever. Except I've never really talked about or watched a video about the game theory of Minecraft. What is there to say? It would be hard to try to analyze that granularly.

I guess you start with the block. Or, actually you probably start with the initial concept, and then talk about how game creation choices in that 'engine' changed the core survival gameplay loop.

To me, it's survive and build, and then there are optional "quest objectives", just not many, like the end, and the 'trial chambers' (boo. I think everything added after caves and cliffs is lame. And honestly idk how I feel about caves and cliffs, I knew some people who were INTO IT tho)

So survive but everything is blocks except non-blocks like grass and leaves and mobs I guess and the player. But other than that a lot of the world is just blocks, each thing is a block and it's all on a 3d grid, which means the 'building blocks' of the world are all the same size a

You know, honestly, after playing for probably over 100 hours on multiple multiplayer servers, trying speedrunning, playing with myself, playing on Xbox and PC (both Bedrock and Java), achievement hunting, beating the game multiple times, I have to say... I'm not the biggest Minecraft fan.

I don't know what it is. I just don't find it compelling.

Hero shooters, I find incredibly compelling ... and by hero shooters I mean overwatch. Can't get into tf2. Can't get into anything that has a 3rd person perspective.

You know, I like general game theory / design stuff coming from a quality YouTube channel, but maybe when it comes to me I just like getting into the design and theory of games I already like. Like when I played Magic, or when I played Overwatch. And sometimes I would buy a little artsie single player game like Obra Dinn when a critic (Like Ben Croshaw) would heap praise on it, and I would enjoy it. Or cuphead (cuz Dunkey). But for the most part, I don't like analyzing rando games.

And I've never been able to finish many games. I usually go back to atmospheric, comforting games. My comfort zones tended to be multiplayer games, like halo custom multiplayer with friends or Overwatch. And the thing about me is, I'm happiest when I'm crushing. I actually do enjoy a good tight match now and then, or if it's truly even and enjoying myself maybe, but for the most part, I just love it when I'm crushing.

And also I do like smash, but sometimes it's not the most satisfying. And when I'm getting crushed ... Multiplayer games suck.

I guess I've never played a PvE game I've enjoyed. Minecraft, D&D (as a player), lol whatever that extraction shooter was.

HEY! I actually like battle royales. I actually enjoyed crushing pubs in fortnite with friends, and I LOVED Apex. Xbox controller. Movement. Respawn.

And also if you lose it's no big deal cuz that's the expectation and then you're just back in a match.

Never played PubG tho. Those hyper realistic games are sometimes brutal and unforgiving, which makes them less fun. I think I wouldn't enjoy it.

Never really played an extraction shooter.

Phasmo and Lethal Company are BORING. Omg I got so bored with them.

I wish I enjoyed league, but it's drawn out. and you gotta learn it. and deal with the people. Yeah, no. No MOBAs.

I'm just going over all the stuff I don't like lol.

Well, ok, I like a good Battle Royale.

And I guess the only tcg I like anymore is Hearthstone. But even then ... araaargh. Card games are so disappointing. Like, I'm over the collecting, leveling up is fun when the rewards seem worth it but that's over in the first like day or two. Netdecking and 'high-level play' are not worth it to me. It's not fun to paint the side of the coin. It's so frustrating. And it squeezes the fun out. But 'quickplay' with different decks isn't all that fun in hearthstone **shrug**

But yeah, Battle Royales are cool. Big gaming rig with a relevant big game - or even Valorant if it wasn't a tac shooter - maybe tac shooters isn't a good idea -

But yeah the dream is a big relevant good-looking game like a battle royale or something fun where I can win and do good and losing doesn't really sting. Like fortnite, I guess. Hyper-competitive games aren't too hard for me to get good at - I can get good at any game I decide to get good at - it's just that the pleasure isn't worth the effort. The juice isn't worth the squeeze.
