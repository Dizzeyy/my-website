One in the Chamber

November 10, 2026

One in the chamber is a wager match game type from call of duty.
It is awesome.
It is one of my favorites.
It probably came from custom game mods on CS:GO servers, which I think is where gun game started.
Here is how one in the chamber works.

First of all, holy shit, what a name for a game type.
Like,
It's so hard to give things a name that sounds good and is thematic and that is just...
Unf.

Anyway,

Here is how one in the chamber works, everyone has a pistol.
And everyone has a knife.
And the pistol has a single bullet.
And it takes one shot,
anywhere in the body,
to kill someone.

And if you kill someone with a knife OR a pistol,
you get
+
1
bullet.

That's it.

In the original call of duty game where it came out, you had points you used to buy cosmetics and stuff,
and you could wager those points on a match and if you lost, you lost the points but if you came in
1st, 2nd, or 3rd, you would win more.

Actually I don't know if the placements were exactly that, it might have been different and there might have been
specific game types or challenges where payouts were different,
but you get the picture.

You put stakes on the game so there's more tension,
and then you get a pistol.

Now you have a game where being able to shoot at a guy three times is a huge deal.

And if someone shoots at you multiple times and misses multiple times,
it's a great feeling,
because you just got lucky.

And if they run out of ammo and they are any amount of paces away from being point blank,
you can shoot at them and all they can do is run in a direction.

It's cool on paper,
it's stressful, it's fun,
and I probably like it the most because of the name.

Anyway, let's analyze a bit of how playing one in the chamber changes how the multiplayer game dynamics work.

First of all, people shoot less.
Second of all, people aim more.
People with excellent aim are rewarded more here, just like in halo's SWAT game types, you would be rewarded more for getting incredibly fast headshots.

Those three changes are drastic.

What you get are killcams where a hiding player will aim down sights and very carefully line up shots on unsuspecting runners.

What else you get is very good at quick aiming, or 'snapping' as some people call it.
Specifically with pistols.

You also focus on a unique gameplay mechanic that is not focused on or explored in other multiplayer game modes, and that is seeing if you can not shit your pants when you have three bullets and you face off against someone else who also has three bullets.

At the end of the day, one in the chamber forces players to slow down and try hard during every encounter if they're going to succeed.

You have one shot unless you get a kill.

Getting people in a shooter game to slow down and try hard every time there is a scuffle is hard.

Usually people push harder at the end of a match, especially when it's close.

But with one in the chamber,
and this is conjecture,
I believe that the effort per minute of each player is higher than average effort in TDM matches.

Each confrontation is a big deal. Someone missing a shot on you is a thrill.
You see someone else and it's like a 'ho SHIT' moment.
It's cool.

Giving players a single lethal bullet increases the stakes,
and making it a wager match increases the stakes,
and as long as the maps in the rotation aren't too large,
then booyah.
